# Loom Walkthrough Script — Dollar Loan Center SEO Assessment

**Runtime target:** 8–10 minutes
**Tabs to have open before recording:**
1. `dontbebroke.com` (homepage)
2. `seobility.net/en/seocheck/` (free SEO checker — no signup needed)
3. `pagespeed.web.dev` (Google PageSpeed Insights)
4. `ubersuggest` or Google Keyword Planner (for the keyword section)
5. A Google search tab

> Speak naturally — this is a guide, not a teleprompter. [Bracketed lines] are what to do on screen.

---

## 1. Intro (0:00–0:45)

[Screen on the Dollar Loan Center homepage]

"Hey, thanks for watching. I'm Michael, and in the next few minutes I'm going to walk through my SEO assessment of the Dollar Loan Center website. I focused on one page — the homepage — because it holds the most authority and it's where the highest-intent visitors land.

My process here was simple: look at the page the way Google sees it, run it through a couple of free SEO tools to confirm what I suspected, and then prioritize the five changes I believe would move the needle most — not a laundry list, just the ones that matter."

---

## 2. First look at the page (0:45–2:00)

[Scroll the homepage slowly]

"So first, just looking at the page as a user: the design is clean, the calls to action are strong — 'Apply Now' is everywhere it should be. The 'Why You Win' section is actually a goldmine: lowest rates guaranteed, funding in minutes, no application fees, no checking account required. Those are real differentiators.

But here's the SEO problem — most of that value is locked in images and short blurbs. There's very little crawlable text answering the questions borrowers actually type into Google: *how much can I borrow, how fast do I get money, do I need a bank account, will they check my credit?*

[Right-click → View Page Source, or Ctrl+U. Ctrl+F for `<title>`]

Now let's look at what Google sees. The title tag is 'Quick & Easy Online Loans' — that's a head term where they're competing with every national fintech in the country. And notice their actual product — the **signature loan** — isn't in the title, the meta description, or really the H1, which reads 'Online and Cash Loans – Dollar Loan Center.'

[Ctrl+F for `og:title`]

And one small but telling thing — the og:title is literally the word 'Home.' That's what shows up when this page gets shared or cited by an AI tool."

---

## 3. Free tool #1 — Seobility SEO Checker (2:00–3:30)

[Switch to seobility.net/en/seocheck, paste dontbebroke.com, run it]

"To sanity-check this, I'm using Seobility's free SEO checker — no account needed, it just crawls the page and grades it.

[While it loads] What I'm looking for here isn't the overall score — those are always a little arbitrary — it's the specific flags.

[When results load, point at the relevant sections]

And it confirms the pattern: the meta and heading structure is generic, the text-to-page ratio is low, and the structured data is thin — the page only outputs basic WebPage schema. For a lender with eighty-plus physical branches and a live Trustpilot profile, there's no LocalBusiness or FinancialService markup and no review ratings exposed to Google at all. That's local-pack visibility they've already earned and just aren't claiming."

---

## 4. Free tool #2 — PageSpeed Insights (3:30–4:15)

[Switch to pagespeed.web.dev, run dontbebroke.com]

"Quick check on Core Web Vitals with Google's own free tool, PageSpeed Insights.

[When it loads] Performance isn't the emergency here — [read the actual numbers on screen honestly, whatever they are] — so I'm deliberately *not* making speed one of my five recommendations. Part of this exercise is prioritization, and content and structure will pay off more than shaving milliseconds."

---

## 5. Keyword research (4:15–5:45)

[Switch to Ubersuggest / Keyword Planner. Search "signature loans"]

"Now the keyword side. This is where the strategy gets interesting.

'Online loans' and 'personal loans' are dominated by NerdWallet, LendingTree, and the big banks — DLC won't win those from a homepage. But 'signature loans' — their literal product — has real volume with much weaker competition, because most national players don't use that term.

[Search a couple more: "payday loan alternative", "loans without a checking account"]

The five keywords I'd target on the homepage: **signature loans**, **online installment loans**, **payday loan alternative**, **same day loans online**, and **loans without a checking account**. That last one is my favorite — almost no competitor can honestly claim it, and anyone typing it is pre-qualified by their own search.

[Optional: Google "signature loans las vegas" and show the local results]

Geo terms like 'signature loans Las Vegas' belong on their state pages — which exist! — the homepage just links to them with empty anchors like 'Find A Location Near You.' Fixing that anchor text is free authority for the pages that convert best."

---

## 6. The five recommendations (5:45–7:30)

[Back on the homepage, or show the assessment PDF]

"So, my five prioritized recommendations:

**One — rewrite the H1** to 'Signature Loans Online: Fast Cash, No Checking Account Required.' Product plus the two differentiators nobody else can match.

**Two — fix the title tag and OG metadata.** My proposed title: 'Signature Loans Online | Cash in Minutes | Dollar Loan Center.' And kill that og:title of 'Home.'

**Three — add an FAQ block** answering the five questions borrowers actually search, marked up with FAQPage schema. That's long-tail rankings, rich results, and AI Overview citations from one block of content.

**Four — upgrade the structured data** to FinancialService and LocalBusiness with their Trustpilot rating, and show the reviews on-page. The trust already exists; it's just invisible to crawlers.

**Five — descriptive internal links** from the homepage to the state pages: 'Signature loans in Las Vegas, NV' instead of 'Find a location.'"

---

## 7. Content rewrite + AI search bonus (7:30–9:00)

[Show the blog rewrite in the PDF]

"For the content piece, I rewrote their 'Same Day Loan Options' blog post — you can see the full 260-word version in the PDF. The approach: lead with the emergency the reader is actually in, compare the four loan options honestly in plain language, and let the signature loan win on facts — no collateral, no checking account, installment repayment. Then trust signals — since 1998, 80-plus locations — and a clear same-day call to action.

Last thing, the bonus question — AI search. AI Overviews and ChatGPT cite pages that answer a question in the first two sentences under a clear heading. So the single biggest opportunity is publishing direct, quotable answers — 'What is a signature loan? A signature loan is an installment loan that requires only your signature and proof of income' — backed by matching schema. DLC is licensed, reviewed, and 25 years old; consistent entity data makes them exactly the kind of source AI engines want to cite for financial queries."

"That's the assessment: five prioritized fixes, five keywords, a rewritten homepage title, meta and H1, a rewritten blog post, and an AI-search play — all focused on the same idea: Dollar Loan Center should stop competing for 'online loans' and start owning 'signature loans.' Everything's in the attached PDF. Thanks for watching."
