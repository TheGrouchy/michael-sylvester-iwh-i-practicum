## Part 1: SEO Review — Homepage (5 Highest-Impact Improvements)

I focused on the homepage because it carries the most authority, ranks for the brand, and is the entry point for the highest-intent visitors. Recommendations are ordered by expected impact.

### 1. Rewrite the H1 around the actual product and its differentiators

**What:** Change the current H1 — "Online and Cash Loans - Dollar Loan Center" — to something like:
> **"Signature Loans Online: Fast Cash, No Checking Account Required"**

**Why:** The current H1 spends half its weight repeating the brand name (already in the title tag and logo) and targets "cash loans," a generic term dominated by national aggregators. DLC's real product is the **signature loan** — a differentiated, lower-competition term with clear commercial intent. The H1 is the strongest on-page relevance signal; it should name the product and lead with the two benefits competitors can't match (speed, no checking account required).

### 2. Fix the title tag and social/OG metadata

**What:** Replace "Quick & Easy Online Loans | Dollar Loan Center" with a product-led title (see Part 3). Also fix `og:title`, which is currently literally **"Home"** — that's what shows when the page is shared or cited.

**Why:** "Quick & Easy Online Loans" competes head-on with national fintechs on a term DLC is unlikely to win, while failing to capture searches for "signature loans" or "installment loans," where DLC has genuine authority. The og:title of "Home" wastes every social share and looks broken when AI tools cite the page.

### 3. Add crawlable, intent-answering content with an FAQ block

**What:** Add a short on-page FAQ section (5–6 questions: *How much can I borrow? How fast is funding? Do I need a checking account? Will you check my credit? What do I need to apply?*) marked up with `FAQPage` structured data.

**Why:** The homepage is visually strong but content-thin — the answers to the questions borrowers actually search are buried on subpages. An FAQ block (a) captures long-tail question queries, (b) is eligible for rich results, and (c) is exactly the format Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT pull answers from. Low effort, compounding return.

### 4. Upgrade structured data and surface reviews on-page

**What:** The homepage currently only outputs generic `WebPage` schema. Add `FinancialService` / `LocalBusiness` schema (per-branch for the 80+ locations) with `aggregateRating` pulled from their existing Trustpilot profile, and show review snippets on the page itself.

**Why:** For "loans near me"-type searches, local pack visibility is won with entity data. DLC already has the trust assets (Trustpilot reviews, state licenses, 25+ years in business) — they're just invisible to crawlers. Star ratings in the SERP also lift click-through rate on every query, not just new rankings.

### 5. Use keyword-rich internal links to state and city pages

**What:** Replace generic homepage anchors like "Find A Location Near You" and "Discover How Loans Work" with descriptive links such as "Signature loans in Las Vegas, NV" or "How signature loans work in Utah," pointing at the existing state pages.

**Why:** DLC already has state-specific pages — the highest-converting local intent lives there ("signature loans las vegas"). But the homepage, the site's strongest page, passes them authority through empty anchor text. Descriptive anchors tell Google exactly what those pages should rank for, at zero content cost.

**Signature loans** need only your signature and proof of income — no collateral, no checking account required — and you repay in manageable installments instead of one lump sum.

## Bonus: AI Search Visibility

The single biggest opportunity: **publish direct, quotable answers with matching structured data.** AI Overviews and ChatGPT cite pages that answer a question in the first two sentences under a clear heading. DLC should add a concise Q&A block ("What is a signature loan? A signature loan is an installment loan that requires only your signature and proof of income — no collateral or checking account.") to the homepage and key state pages, marked up with `FAQPage` and `FinancialService` schema, and fix the `og:title="Home"` issue so citations display properly. Because DLC's licensing, Trustpilot rating, and 25+ year history are verifiable, consistent entity data makes them exactly the kind of source AI engines prefer to cite for regulated financial queries.
